Standardisation of the institutional affiliation and
personal name in publications by people linked to
Universidade de Vigo
Subject matter
One of the objectives of Universidade de Vigo, as stated in its Statutes, is to create,
develop, evaluate, and transmit knowledge through quality and excellence in teaching
and research. Moreover, Article 5 states that the university will ensure appropriate
monitoring and regular evaluation of its activities.
Scientific and artistic publications are a fundamental element in the dissemination of
research results. Therefore, analysing their impact and visibility makes it possible to
appreciate the projection and scope of the research carried out by members of the
university.
The aim of this Protocol is to standardise the name of the institution and its members
in publications and works of art. In this way, it will be easier for such publications to
be assigned without error in the sources of information and in the various tools for
evaluating scientific production. This standardisation will increase the reliability of the
evaluation indicators and improve the visibility of the scientific production of university
members.

People to whom this Protocol applies
•

Academic and research staff, research trainees, administrative and service staff
(permanent or temporary), and students of Universidade de Vigo in publications and
works related to the work carried out within Universidade de Vigo through the use of
its resources, facilities, and services.

•

Members of foundations, research institutions, technology centres, and other
entities related to Universidade de Vigo that currently exist or will be created in the
future.

•

Members of other universities and institutions who are on a stay at Universidade de
Vigo for all publications totally or partially related to the work carried out during their
stay.

Legal foundations
•

Act 14/2011 of 1 June, on Science, Technology, and Innovation. Article 15
includes, among the obligations of researchers, the use of the name of the entities
to which they provide services in the exercise of their scientific activities, in
accordance with the internal Regulations of those entities and the Agreements,
Pacts, and Conventions they have signed.

•

Decree 13/2019, of 24 January, approving the Statutes of Universidade de Vigo.
Article 4 establishes that the official name of the university is “Universidade de
Vigo”.

•

Universidade de Vigo Groups, Associations, and Research Centres Regulations
(approved on 26 June 2019). Article 5 states that all people registered in a
research group or as individual researchers undertake to mention Universidade de
Vigo in any type of research or transfer production resulting from their activity.

•

Industrial and Intellectual Property Regulations of Universidade de Vigo (19
June 2012). Article 7 states that in any case and unless otherwise specified by
Universidade de Vigo, any invention or work in which members of the university
are involved and which is produced as a direct or indirect consequence of their
academic, teaching, or research duties shall indicate their affiliation with the
aforementioned institution.

•

Regulations on the use of Galician at Universidade de Vigo (approved by the
Galician Minister Council on 2 March 2015). Article 1, Point 1.4, indicates that
the official name of the university is Universidade de Vigo, and must be stated as
such. The names of Centres, Studies, Departments, Institutes, and other research
institutions, as well as university or administrative services, shall be in Galician.

•

Corporate Identity Manual of Universidade de Vigo. Section 2.1 states that the
name of the institution is Universidade de Vigo and its translation into other
languages is not permitted.

•

Proposal for a manual to help Spanish researchers to standardise the names of
authors and institutions in scientific publications (version 17/01/07). This manual
is a document prepared for the FECYT by the research groups EC3 of Universidad
de Granada and Quantitative analysis in Science and Technology of CINDOCCSIC.

Protocol for the standardisation of the scientific signature

Guidelines for the standardisation of the
institutional name
Name of the institution
How to include the name of the institution
In all publications and works in which a member of Universidade de Vigo is involved,
the name of the university must appear in the signature of the work. In addition, if
the signatory is part of a Strategic Group, Research Institute or Research Centre of
Universidade de Vigo, the name of said Centre, Institute or Group must be stated. If
the member does not belong to a Strategic Group, Institute or Research Centre, the
research group or department may be used as a secondary unit.
Form of the name of the institution
Only the official form “Universidade de Vigo” may be used. It must be written in
Galician, without any acronyms or abbreviations. If it is necessary to add the name
in English, the form “University of Vigo” must be used, which in no case replaces the
official name.
Names of Departments, Institutes, Groups, and Research Centres
The names of Centres, Strategic Groups, Departments, Institutes, and Groups
must use the official form, full name, abbreviation or both, as determined by each
institution.

Elements of the signature and their order
As a general rule, the name of Universidade de Vigo must appear first in the affiliation
section. If the signatory is part of a Research Centre, Research Institute or Strategic
Group of Universidade de Vigo, the name of that Centre, Research Institute or Group
should appear first, followed immediately by the name of Universidade de Vigo. Any
reference to other entities, such as Groups, Departments or Campuses, should follow.
The different elements of the signature must be separated by a comma and must
always be within the same institutional affiliation. Lastly, the postal address should be
added, including the postal code, city, and country. In the case of including an email
address, the institutional email address provided by Universidade de Vigo must be
used.

Multiple affiliations
If a person is affiliated to more than one institution, it is recommended that they
include a dual affiliation in their work. In addition, Universidade de Vigo and its
Institutes, Centres, Departments, and Groups must be mentioned first, as established
in this Protocol. If the other institution to which the researcher belongs is a mixed
centre or a foreign centre to Universidade de Vigo, the dual affiliation must be
indicated separately.
Researchers from Universidade de Vigo must always indicate their dual affiliation
separately in all their publications during their stays in other institutions: Universidade
de Vigo and the institution where they are staying.
Likewise, members of other institutions who are temporarily at Universidade de Vigo
must acknowledge the Universidade de Vigo in their affiliation in all publications
relating in whole or in part to the work carried out during their stay, as specified in this
Protocol.

Recommendation for the standardisation of the
personal name
Using a single form of personal signature for all works and publications makes it easier to associate them all with the same person, both in databases and in tools for the
Evaluation of Scientific Production. Therefore, it is important that each person chooses a single form of signature that remains the same over time.
Moreover, it is necessary for members of Universidade de Vigo to have an ORCID
number that identifies all their publications.

Forename
In the case of simple forenames, it is advisable to use the full name, not the abbreviation. In composite forenames, the best option is to reduce the middle name to its
initial to avoid confusion with the surname. Other options are to join all parts of the
name with hyphens or to delete one of the parts of the name.
Examples:

Carme

María C.

Ana-Beatriz

Surname
In many linguistic fields only one surname is used even if you have two, since using
both surnames in the signature often leads to confusion in databases and publishing
houses, which usually consider the last surname to be the only one. Therefore, it is
advisable to use both surnames separated by a hyphen, or only one surname if it is
uncommon. The surname written in this way combined with the first name will clearly
identify the signatory.
Similarly, if the surname contains prepositions, it is advisable to eliminate them.
If it is preferred to keep them, they should be joined to the surname with a hyphen.
The graphic accents of forenames and surnames must be preserved and their abbreviations must never be used.

Personal identifiers
There are personal identifiers that allow all publications by the same author to be
grouped together, even if the personal name appears in different forms.
ORCID (Open Researcher and Contribution ID) is an international non-profit organisation that provides a unique and persistent digital identifier that can be linked to a
personal profile. Said identifier allows to group together all publications. It is currently
the most widely used identifier and it is demanded by many scientific publishers. It is
the identifier that must be used so that the scientific production database of Universidade de Vigo can be updated correctly.
Other personal identifiers:
•

Scopus e Web of Science proporcionan rexistros de autor/a baixo un identificador
único (Scopus Author ID and ResearcherID), que aglutinan todas as publicacións
malia que orixinalmente fosen asinadas de xeitos diferentes.

•

Google Scholar ofrece a posibilidade de crear un perfil de autor/a público que
recolle todas as publicacións independentemente de como tiñan sido asinadas.
